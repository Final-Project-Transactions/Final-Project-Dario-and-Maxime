## 🔭 We are currently working on: [Social Spending](https://github.com/Final-Project-Transactions/final-project-dario-and-maxime)

**How does my spending compare to my friends, my groups and everyone else?**

Social Spending is a web app we built for the course *Data Analysis with Python* (UZH, HS26).
Banking apps show how much you spend, but not whether that is a lot. Our app lets users compare
their monthly spending with friends, groups and all other users, while individual purchases stay private.

**What the app does**


📊 See how much you spend, where your money goes and how your spending changes each month

➕ Add cash payments or upload a bank file. The app sorts your payments into categories for you

👯 Add friends and create groups, for example with your flatmates

⚖️ See how your monthly spending compares with friends, a group or all users using the average, median and percentile

🔒 Your password is protected with bcrypt, and other users only see your monthly totals

**Data:** 150 simulated users in Zurich over 12 months (about 94,500 transactions), based on six
lifestyles from students to retirees and on official Swiss figures. Real bank data is private,
so the data is simulated.

**Built with:** Python · Streamlit · pandas · Plotly · Neon (database) · bcrypt
